How We Work

From Bare Hall
To Certified Cinema

Every project follows the same disciplined process — whether it's a single screen upgrade or a 12-screen multiplex build.

01
Site Visit & Acoustic Assessment

We measure the hall, assess seating capacity, screen size, and existing acoustic treatment. Every recommendation starts with real measurements, not guesswork.

02
System Design & Equipment Spec

Based on hall dimensions and budget, we design the channel layout and specify exact speaker, amplifier, and processor models — CA-series or premium third-party brands.

03
Manufacturing & Procurement

CA-series speakers are built to order in our facility. Third-party equipment is sourced and quality-checked before it ever reaches your site.

04
Installation & Wiring

Our certified engineers handle mounting, cable routing, amplifier rack setup, and all physical installation work to industry standards.

05
Calibration & Room Tuning

Processor programming, crossover alignment, and SPL calibration specific to your room's acoustic signature. This is where a good system becomes a great one.

06
Handover & Ongoing AMC

We don't disappear after installation. Annual maintenance contracts keep your system performing at spec, with rapid response when something needs attention.

Maintenance

AMC Plans That
Actually Respond

A cinema with a broken sound system is a cinema that's cancelling shows and losing revenue. Our Annual Maintenance Contracts are built around one priority: rapid response.

We offer both comprehensive and non-comprehensive AMC structures depending on your equipment age and budget — covering amplifier repairs, sound system repairs, and processor servicing.

Comprehensive AMC
Full coverage including parts, labour, and emergency callouts. Best for ageing systems or operators who want zero surprise costs.
Non-Comprehensive AMC
Scheduled preventive visits and labour included; parts billed separately. Cost-effective for newer, reliable installations.
Emergency Response
Priority response windows for AMC clients. A silent screen on a Friday night gets our immediate attention — not a queue position.
